# Jeevan Cars — Admin guide

For the person managing the website day to day. Sign in at **your-domain/admin**.

## Signing in

- Use the email and password set up for you. After 5 wrong passwords the account pauses sign-in for 15 minutes.
- You stay signed in for up to 7 days of inactivity (30 days at most). Use **Sign out** on shared computers.
- Lost a phone or laptop? **Settings › Sign out everywhere** ends every session.

## Dashboard

A quick view of stock (available, reserved, sold, drafts), new enquiries and what was added recently. Click any number to jump to the matching list.

## Adding a vehicle

**Vehicles › Add vehicle.** The form is split into sections:

1. **Photos** — drag photos in or click _Add photos_ (JPG, PNG, WebP or AVIF, up to 10 MB each, up to 30 per vehicle). Drag or use the arrows to reorder; click the star to choose the **cover** photo shown on listings and when shared on WhatsApp. Add a short description to each photo — it helps search engines and visually impaired visitors.
2. **Basics** — category, brand, model and variant. Leave _Listing title_ blank to use "Brand Model Variant" automatically. Write an honest description: condition, service history, recent work, features.
3. **Specifications** — year, kilometres, fuel, gearbox, owners, condition, colour, seats. Use **Extra details** for anything else, e.g. _Commercial › Payload › 1,240 kg_ or _Features › Sunroof › Yes_.
4. **Private details** — registration number and internal notes. These are **never** shown on the website.
5. **Publishing** — _Draft_ keeps the vehicle hidden; _Published_ shows it on the site (needs at least one photo). Set availability to Available, Reserved or Sold. Turn on _Feature on homepage_ for your best stock.
6. **Pricing** — enter the selling price. Turn off _Show price publicly_ to display "Contact for Price" instead; the price is then kept private.

You'll be warned if you try to leave with unsaved changes.

## Day-to-day changes

From **Vehicles**, use the **⋯** menu on any row to mark a vehicle **Sold** / **Reserved** / **Available**, publish or unpublish it, feature it, or delete it. Sold vehicles leave the catalogue but their page stays online with a "sold" notice, so links people shared still work.

Prefer **Sold** or **Unpublish** over **Delete** — deleting removes the listing and its photos permanently. Enquiries about a deleted vehicle are kept.

## Enquiries

Every enquiry from the website appears in **Enquiries**, newest first, with a badge in the sidebar for new ones.

- Open an enquiry to **call**, **WhatsApp** (a reply is pre-written) or **email** the customer.
- Move it through the stages: **New → Contacted → Follow-up → Converted / Closed**.
- Add private **notes** and a **follow-up date** — the list shows upcoming follow-ups.
- Delete only spam or duplicates.

## Categories and brands

Add or rename them from **Categories** and **Brands**. A category or brand that vehicles still use can't be deleted — hide it instead (it stays usable in the admin but disappears from the website).

## Settings

- **Business, contact, address and hours** — appear in the footer, Contact page and WhatsApp/call buttons.
- **Google Maps** — in Google Maps, choose _Share › Embed a map_ and paste only the link inside `src="…"`.
- **Homepage hero** — a wide landscape photo (at least 2400 px across) with the subject on the right; the headline sits on the left.
- **Password** — change it here; other devices are signed out automatically.

Changes appear on the website immediately after saving.
